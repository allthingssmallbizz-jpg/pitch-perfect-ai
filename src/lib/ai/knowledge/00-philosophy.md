# Pitch Perfect AI™ — Core Philosophy (Playbook 1)

Source: *The Pitch Perfect Philosophy™*. Condensed for use as generation reference context.

## The Pitch Perfect Workflow™ (7 phases, applies to every asset)

1. **Discovery** — understand the business, customer, market, competitors, offer, and evidence before writing anything.
2. **Strategy** — turn Discovery into a Discovery Brief, Customer Decision Profile, Positioning Analysis, Transformation Mapping, and Confidence Audit.
3. **Positioning** — decide how the offer is framed relative to alternatives and the status quo.
4. **Presentation Design** — build the actual asset (webinar, VSL, sales page, etc.) using Decision Architecture™.
5. **Offer Development** — build/validate the Offer Blueprint (core offer, bonuses, guarantee, pricing, risk reversal).
6. **Quality Review** — run the asset against the 50-Point Quality Review before it ships.
7. **Campaign Expansion** — repurpose the core asset into supporting assets (emails, ads, follow-ups).

**Rule: never skip to Phase 4 (writing) without Phases 1–3 (Discovery → Strategy → Positioning).** This is the "discovery-before-copy" rule referenced throughout Pitch Perfect AI.

## The Seven Pillars (persuasion foundation — see PPOS below)

Every persuasive asset Pitch Perfect AI produces is judged against these Seven Pillars:
Clarity, Relevance, Credibility, Evidence, Emotional Resonance, Logical Structure, and a Clear Next Step.

## Professional Standards

- No claim without evidence. If evidence doesn't exist yet, the claim is flagged as a hypothesis, not stated as fact.
- Customer language over company language wherever possible.
- Every section of every asset must justify its place — if it doesn't move a belief or reduce uncertainty, cut it.
- Outline-approval rule: for longer assets (webinars, VSLs, sales pages), a strategist should confirm the outline/structure before full copy is generated.

## The 50-Point Quality Review (abridged categories)

Discovery completeness · Positioning clarity · Offer strength · Belief architecture · Evidence sufficiency · Emotional pacing · Structural logic · CTA clarity · Objection coverage · Overall persuasive momentum.
