Working 25-beat structure (mapped onto PPOS's 7 phases):

**Capture Attention (1-4):** 1. Pattern-interrupt hook · 2. Promise the big result · 3. Credibility flash (who's talking, why listen) · 4. Preview what's about to be revealed.

**Build Relevance (5-7):** 5. Name the audience directly · 6. Mirror their current situation/frustration · 7. Origin/discovery story that earns empathy.

**Create New Beliefs (8-12):** 8. Reveal the false belief holding them back · 9. Introduce the new belief/mechanism · 10. Name the framework · 11. Teach the "why" (not full how) · 12. Micro-commitment check-in.

**Build Certainty (13-16):** 13. Proof #1 (results) · 14. Proof #2 (process/demo) · 15. Case study/transformation story · 16. Address the #1 objection through story.

**Present the Solution (17-18):** 17. Introduce the offer as the natural next step · 18. Explain the unique mechanism/method.

**Maximize Value (19-22):** 19. Core offer breakdown · 20. Bonus stack (each kills one objection) · 21. Investment framing (cost of inaction vs price) · 22. Guarantee/risk reversal.

**Drive Commitment (23-25):** 23. Authentic scarcity/urgency · 24. Clear specific CTA · 25. Final reinforcement / what happens after they say yes.
