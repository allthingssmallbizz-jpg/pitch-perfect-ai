# Sales Presentation Playbook™ (Playbook 9) — PPSOS, condensed

For enterprise/B2B decks and PowerPoint outlines with multiple stakeholders. Same PPOS psychology, executive execution.

## Stakeholder Alignment Matrix™
CEO → strategic growth/competitive advantage · CFO → ROI/cost control · COO → efficiency/execution · CIO/CTO → integration/security · Operations → simplicity/reliability · Procurement → fair pricing/manageable risk · End Users → usability/support.

## The Seven Phases of PPSOS™

1. **Capture Executive Attention™** — open with business trends/strategic opportunity, not company history. State the meeting objective and expected business value in the first minute.
2. **Build Business Relevance™** — Business Diagnosis (current vs desired state), quantified Cost of Inaction, multi-stakeholder framing, opportunity framing (not just problem framing).
3. **Create New Business Beliefs™** — reframe the industry context, introduce a named strategic framework, respectfully challenge one false assumption, teach concepts not product features.
4. **Build Executive Certainty™** — Business Case (ROI/efficiency/growth), comparable case studies, outcome-focused demo (not feature tour), risk management (implementation/training/integration), executive-grade proof.
5. **Present the Solution™** — position as "the approach we recommend" (consultant framing, not a pitch), Solution Architecture, Business Transformation arc, competitive differentiation without attacking competitors, future vision (6mo/1yr/3yr).
6. **Maximize Business Value™** — Executive Value Equation (outcomes + revenue + cost reduction + efficiency + risk reduction + strategic advantage + support ≫ investment), realistic ROI, Total Cost of Ownership transparency, implementation roadmap, executive support/partnership.
7. **Drive Organizational Commitment™** — Decision Roadmap (what happens next, who's involved, timeline), stakeholder-specific next steps, procurement readiness (pricing/security/compliance), resolve common exec objections (budget/timing/resources/change mgmt), CTA matched to buying stage (workshop/pilot/proposal — not "buy now"), close on partnership.

## Use this framework for
The "PPT / slide outline" generator when the project reads as B2B/enterprise, and any "sales page" copy targeted at a business buyer rather than a consumer.
