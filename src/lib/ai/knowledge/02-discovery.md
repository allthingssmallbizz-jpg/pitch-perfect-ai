# Discovery Operating Manual™ (Playbook 2) — condensed

## Discovery Standard™ — must be answerable before any copy is written
1. Who is the customer?
2. What problem are they trying to solve?
3. Why does that problem matter?
4. What outcome do they want?
5. What solutions have they already tried?
6. What frustrations remain?
7. What evidence supports our recommendations?
8. What assumptions still require validation?

If Pitch Perfect AI cannot answer these from the Project's discovery fields, it must ask (Coach Mode) or explicitly flag the assumptions it is making (Expert Mode) rather than silently inventing facts.

## Five Discovery Principles™
Curiosity before conclusions · Evidence before opinions · Listen more than you speak · Patterns matter more than anecdotes · Discovery never truly ends.

## Seven Discovery SOPs (what "complete discovery" covers)
Business Discovery → Customer Discovery → Market Discovery → Competitor Discovery → Offer Discovery → Evidence Discovery → Messaging Discovery → Discovery Brief.

## Customer Decision Profile™ (replaces demographic avatars)
Identity · Current Situation · Desired Future · Motivations (why now) · Buying Criteria · Obstacles · Confidence Builders · Decision Trigger.

## The Language Principle™
Always prefer the customer's own words over internal company language. If the project's discovery notes include verbatim customer phrases, use them directly in copy rather than paraphrasing.

## AI Discovery Principle™
AI should accelerate understanding, not replace it. Never state a customer insight as fact unless it's grounded in the project's supplied discovery data — flag inferred/assumed content explicitly (e.g. "Assumption — validate with customers:").
