# Perfect Webinar Operating Manual™ (Playbook 8) — PPWOS, condensed

Webinars run PPOS live, with more room for teaching, proof, and interaction. Applies to both live and evergreen webinars.

## The Seven Phases of PPWOS™ (webinar-specific execution of PPOS)

| Phase | Audience question | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Capture Attention™ | "Is this worth my time?" | Welcome, Big Promise (outcome not topic), light agenda, immediate engagement trigger (poll/chat) |
| 2. Build Relevance™ | "Is this about someone like me?" | Audience identification, opportunity framing, shared-experience story (Epiphany Bridge™ — Playbook 10), interactive reflection |
| 3. Create New Beliefs™ | "Could there be a better way?" | The Three Secrets™: the Big Domino™ (Playbook 10) broken into its Vehicle, Internal, and External false beliefs, each delivered via its own Epiphany Bridge story, strategic teaching (why not how), false-belief removal ("many people naturally assume... but"), named framework introduction, micro-commitments ("does this make sense?") |
| 4. Build Certainty™ | "Can I trust this approach?" | Demonstrations, case studies, testimonials (each answers a different objection), live examples |
| 5. Present the Solution™ | "Is this the solution I've been looking for?" | Introduce offer as logical conclusion; Unique Method™; future pacing 30/90/365 days |
| 6. Maximize Value™ | "Is this worth the investment?" | The Stack™, revealed in full MULTIPLE times, never once: core offer alone → every stack item/bonus introduced one at a time, each followed immediately by its own running-total recap → a full stack reveal → guarantee/risk reversal + scarcity (why it's limited) — still no price — → a second full stack reveal ("so what exactly are you getting?") → the Fast Action Bonus + more proof — still no price — → a third, final full stack reveal right before the price → the required Value Justification Question, its own beat, worded exactly "Although I'm not going to charge you [total] today, if I were to charge you that, would it be worth it?" — still no price — → only then, the price revealed as a small fraction of that total, on its own slide/beat alone (nothing else shares it) |
| 7. Drive Commitment™ | "Why should I act today?" | Everything here is about the price Phase 6 just revealed: why this price won't last (rises later / a real deadline tied to the Fast Action Bonus's own condition), the Two Paths close (go it alone vs. work with the presenter — see below), objection handling, an emotional close, one clear repeated CTA — the guarantee/scarcity/bonus already ran pre-price in Phase 6, never repeated here |

## PPWOS Operating Rules
Never teach without strategic purpose · every lesson moves ≥1 belief · never reveal offer before confidence exists · every interaction increases investment · Q&A is part of selling, not separate from it · build value before price · close by increasing confidence, not pressure.

## Structure to output for a "Webinar Outline" asset
For each of the 7 phases: a one-line strategic objective + 3-6 bullet beats (welcome/hook, stories, teaching points, proof, transition) + the engagement trigger for that section. Phase 3 names the Big Domino™ once, then breaks it into The Three Secrets™ (Playbook 10) — Vehicle, Internal, External — each with its own false belief and its own dedicated Epiphany Bridge story, not one story covering all three. Phase 6's Stack™ is a HARD RULE phase: the price must not appear anywhere in it, on-slide or in notes, until its very last beat — and that includes any hint about the price: no "the price will go up," "rising to $X after the cohort," or "this price will never be the same" before the real price has been revealed (those are Phase 7 statements; pre-price scarcity is only seats/spots/availability, never a price fact or dollar figure). It's a sequence of beats, not one: core offer alone → every stack item/bonus introduced one at a time, each immediately followed by a beat re-displaying the running stack so far (a pair of beats per item, never a handful of grouped recap beats) → a full stack reveal (every item + total) → the guarantee/risk reversal and scarcity (why it's genuinely limited, never the price) → a second full stack reveal ("so what exactly are you getting?") → the Fast Action Bonus (if one was given) plus more proof — still no price — → a third, final full stack reveal right before the price → the required Value Justification Question, its own dedicated beat, never skipped and never folded into the final reveal or the price beat: states the total value explicitly and asks it word-for-word as "Although I'm not going to charge you [total] today, if I were to charge you that, would it be worth it?" (never paraphrased) — still no price — → only now, the price revealed against that total, shown as the total struck through dropping to the real price — always its own slide/beat alone, never sharing it with the question, guarantee, bonuses, urgency, payment plan, or CTA. How many full reveals this needs scales with the project's own price: 2 under $2,000, 3 between $2,000-$5,000, 4 over $5,000 — never just one, regardless of price. Phase 7 is everything that comes after the price and ONLY after it: why this price won't last (rises later / a real deadline), then the required Two Paths beat — never skipped, never folded into the guarantee: lay out two explicit paths forward — path one, take everything just taught and go try to implement it alone (a genuinely valid choice); path two, work with the presenter/the presenter's team instead, who has already walked this exact path and knows the real blueprint, putting the audience in the strongest position to actually get the result faster and with less trial and error. This is a distinct beat whose whole job is giving explicit permission via a real choice, not another angle on risk removal. Then objection handling, an emotional close, and CTA. End with the Closing Sequence: Transformation Recap → Offer → Value Stack (revealed multiple times) → Guarantee → Scarcity → Bonuses → Value Justification Question → Price → Price Justification → Two Paths → CTA → Q&A → Final Reinforcement.

## Q&A — Three-Part Answer™
Acknowledge → Answer → Reinforce (reconnect the answer to the larger transformation).
