# The Epiphany Bridge & Big Domino Operating Manual (Playbook 10), condensed

How PPOS Phase 3 (Create New Beliefs™) actually gets executed — not as a lecture, but as a
story that delivers the belief shift experientially. Universal: applies to webinars, VSLs,
sales pages, challenges, and enterprise decks alike, anywhere a belief needs to move.

## The Big Domino™

Before writing any belief-shifting content, find the ONE belief that, if truly adopted, makes
every other objection collapse on its own — time, money, "will this work for someone like me,"
past failures, all of it. Don't argue every objection point-by-point; knock down the Big Domino
and the rest fall with it. This IS the "One Belief Method™" already named in PPOS Phase 3/the
Trust Ladder — the Big Domino is that one belief, named explicitly, and the whole asset exists
to knock it down.

Frame the offer as a **New Opportunity** (a new mechanism/vehicle they've never tried) rather
than a **Vehicle Improvement** (a better/faster version of something they've already tried and
judged). New Opportunities bypass "I already tried that" and direct price/feature comparison —
the prospect has nothing to compare it to yet.

## The Epiphany Bridge™ story structure

The replicable story template for delivering a belief shift through story instead of argument —
"show, don't tell": the audience reaches the new belief by living through the moment the
storyteller reached it, not by being told to believe it.

1. **Backstory** — where the storyteller started: relatable, same starting point as the
   audience right now (same doubts, same frustration, same false belief).
2. **Desire** — what they wanted at that point.
3. **The Wall** — what stopped them. Three kinds, often combined: **External** (circumstances,
   resources, other people), **Internal** (self-doubt, lack of skill/confidence), and
   **Philosophical** (a belief they held that turned out to be wrong — this is usually the
   actual Big Domino in disguise).
4. **The Epiphany** — the specific moment/insight that broke through the wall. State this as
   something *realized in the moment*, not asserted as a lesson — this line is the belief being
   planted, delivered as a discovery, not a claim.
5. **The Plan** — what they did differently immediately after the epiphany.
6. **The Conflict** — real obstacles the plan still ran into. Never skip this: a story with no
   friction after the epiphany reads as "too easy to be believable" and undercuts the belief
   shift instead of selling it.
7. **The Achievement** — the result, which retroactively proves the epiphany was true.

## Operating Rules

- Every major belief shift in PPOS Phase 3 (and PPWOS Phase 3 / PPSOS Phase 3) should be carried
  by an Epiphany Bridge story wherever a real story/example is available — not a bare teaching
  point. A "shared-experience story" called for elsewhere in these playbooks (e.g. PPWOS Phase 2
  Build Relevance) should follow this same structure.
- One Epiphany Bridge per belief, not one giant story trying to shift several beliefs at once —
  matches the One Belief Method's "pick ONE dominant belief shift" rule.
- The epiphany line must name the specific realization, not just "and then I figured it out" —
  if discovery data doesn't supply a real story, flag it as `[NEEDS: a real story/example for
  this belief shift]` per the Discovery-before-copy rule rather than inventing one.
